**There IS a new Course and Exam Description effective Fall 2026 for AP World History: Modern.**
verified live 2026-10-02 (multiple corroborating sources):

- The AP Central AP World History course page (crawled 2026-09-09) shows the current framework, skills, and unit weights.

**What changed vs the prior CED (format changes, content framework unchanged):**
verified live 2026-10-02 (corroborated by Albert.io 2027 exam guide, Fiveable SAQ guide, and a TPT 2027 review book):
1. **SAQs:** All 3 SAQs are now required and source-based. The old "answer Q1+Q2 and either Q3 or Q4" choice is gone. Q3 is now a non-text source (map/chart/image) instead of a source-free recall question.
2. **LEQ:** Single required prompt — the old "choose 1 of 3" is gone.
3. **DBQ:** Still 7 documents, but the date range expands from **1450–2001 to 1200–2001**.
4. **Unchanged:** MCQ section, all rubrics (point values), the 9-unit content framework, the 6 themes, the skills.

## 2. Exam format for May 2027

verified live 2026-10-02 (Albert.io 2027 AP World guide, crawled ~14h before research; corroborated by asksia.ai and Fiveable):

| Section | Questions | Time | % of score |
|---|---|---|---|
| I-A: Multiple Choice | 55 questions | 55 minutes | 40% |
| I-B: Short Answer | 3 questions, **all required** | 40 minutes | 20% |
| II-A: Document-Based Question | 1 question, **7 documents** | 60 min (incl. 15-min reading period) | 25% |
| II-B: Long Essay | 1 question, **single prompt** | 40 minutes | 15% |

- **Total:** 3 hours 15 minutes. Fully digital in the Bluebook app (both sections on screen).
- **MCQ:** Stimulus-based sets; no penalty for wrong answers. [UNVERIFIED against new CED]: exact set size — one source cites the 2020 CED as "sets of three or four"; a current prep source says "sets of 2–5." Treat as sets of 2–5 pending CED confirmation.
- **SAQ source types** (verified live 2026-10-02 via Fiveable SAQ guide, crawled 51 days ago):
  - Q1: secondary **text** source stimulus
  - Q2: primary **text** source stimulus
  - Q3: primary or secondary **non-text** source stimulus (map, chart, graph, image, political cartoon)
  - Each SAQ has 3 parts (a/b/c); SAQs span the full course range c. 1200–2001.
- **DBQ:** 7 documents; topic spans **1200–2001** (expanded from 1450–2001).
- **LEQ:** one broad prompt; students choose their own line of reasoning and evidence.

## 4. Rubrics

Point totals confirmed by the official 2025 scoring statistics (7/6/3 possible points); structure is the shared AP history-exam rubric, reported unchanged under the new CED.
verified live 2026-10-02 (2025 AP Central scoring statistics PDFs):

- **DBQ — 7 points:** Thesis/Claim 1 + Contextualization 1 + Evidence 3 (describe ≥3 docs: 1 pt; support argument with ≥6 docs: +1; one additional outside evidence: +1) + Analysis & Reasoning 2 (sourcing ≥3 docs: 1 pt; complex understanding: 1 pt).
- **LEQ — 6 points:** Thesis/Claim 1 + Contextualization 1 + Evidence 2 (≥2 specific examples: 1 pt; examples support argument: +1) + Analysis & Reasoning 2 (targeted reasoning process: 1 pt; complex understanding: 1 pt).
- **SAQ — 3 points each:** 1 point per part (a/b/c); no partial credit within a part. 9 SAQ points total.

[UNVERIFIED]: The per-point breakdown above is the long-standing shared rubric; the 2025 stats confirm only the totals (7/6/3). Confirm no wording changes in the Fall 2026 CED rubric pages.
