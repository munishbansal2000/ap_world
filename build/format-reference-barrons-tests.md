# Format Reference — Barron's Online Tests (New Fall 2026 CED)

Source: `books/barrons-tests/ap-world-history.zip` — 3 Barron's online practice tests
(test1/2/3/questions.json). Each = 55 MCQ + 3 SAQ + 1 DBQ + 1 LEQ.
This is the NEW Fall 2026 format (unlike the Barron's EPUB, which is old-format).
Barron's IP — re-conceive, never copy. Use for FORMAT only.

## SAQ (Section 2 of 4) — 3 required, source-type rotation confirmed

| Q | Type | Directions text | Attribution style |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Secondary text | "Use the passage below to answer all parts of the question that follows." | "—Historian's account of [topic] in the [period]" |
| 2 | Primary text | "Use the passage below to answer all parts of the question that follows." | "—Adapted from [Author], [description], [date]" |
| 3 | Non-text | "Use the map below to answer all parts of the question that follows." / "Use the image below..." | Map/image/chart with caption |

Parts format: `A. Describe ONE... B. Explain ONE... C. Explain ONE...`
Skill verbs observed: Identify, Describe, Explain. Parts are skill-differentiated
(e.g., A = describe a change, B = explain a cause, C = explain a continuity).

## DBQ (Section 3 of 4) — 7 documents

Prompt format: "Using the documents and your knowledge of world history, [prompt]."
Documents: "Document N" header + "Source:" line + excerpt. Mix of text and
non-text (test3 has 3 inline images among its 7 docs).

## LEQ (Section 4 of 4) — single prompt

Shape: "In the period circa [X] to [Y], [one context sentence]. [Prompt question].
Your response may focus on any example(s) of [topic] during the period circa [X]
to [Y]. You are not expected to address the entire time period in your response."

The closing two sentences are the new-format LEQ directions — use them verbatim
in our LEQs (directions text is not copyrightable expression).
